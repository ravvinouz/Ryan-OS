💊 PIH — Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation
Those pink/dark spots on your right cheek are surface pigment left after pimples. NOT deep scarring. 100% fixable. You're already seeing results — keep the routine and they'll be mostly gone in 4–6 more weeks.
What it is
Surface melanin overproduction. Not deep scarring.
Makes worse
Sun, picking, sugar (Mountain Dew), skipping moisturizer.
Fades it
Niacinamide, vitamin C, potato juice, SPF every day.
Timeline
You're week 2. Mostly clear by week 6–8 with consistency.

Your Hair Type — 2B/2C Wavy
Medium-thick strands with natural S-wave pattern. Sits between straight and curly — responds well to defining products and low manipulation. Key: avoid heat, embrace the texture, and use protein-moisture balance to maintain wave definition without frizz.
What works for your type
  • ✓
    Wash with cool or lukewarm water — hot water kills waves
  • ✓
    Scrunch don't rub when drying — towel friction causes frizz
  • ✓
    Leave-in conditioner mid-lengths to ends after every wash
  • ✓
    Oil on dry hair before washing, not after — pre-poo method
  • ✓
    Damon cut = perfect for 2B/2C — textured, not layered flat
  • ✗
    Avoid sulfate shampoos — strips natural oils, kills definition
  • ✗
    No brushing when dry — breaks wave pattern
